Senior NLP / Search Ranking Engineer — Redrob (Pune or Noida, India)

We are hiring a Senior NLP / Search Ranking Engineer to build and improve candidate–job
matching at Redrob. You will design, implement, and productionize search and ranking
systems that help recruiters discover the best talent from large candidate pools.

Responsibilities:
- Build and maintain semantic search and learning-to-rank pipelines over millions of profiles
- Develop embedding-based retrieval using vector databases (FAISS, Milvus, Qdrant, Weaviate, pgvector)
- Implement and tune IR components: BM25, dense retrieval, hybrid search, re-ranking
- Apply modern NLP/LLM tooling: Hugging Face Transformers, sentence embeddings, RAG, LangChain, LlamaIndex
- Fine-tune and deploy models (LoRA, PEFT, QLoRA) for domain-specific matching
- Productionize ML with PyTorch, MLOps tooling (MLflow, Kubeflow, Weights & Biases)
- Collaborate with product and data engineering on Elasticsearch / OpenSearch / Haystack stacks
- Measure ranking quality (NDCG, MRR, precision@k) and iterate on feature and model improvements

Requirements:
- 5–9 years of software / ML engineering experience with strong Python proficiency
- Hands-on experience with information retrieval, recommendation systems, or semantic search
- Production experience with at least one vector database and one search backend
- Solid understanding of embeddings, learning-to-rank, and NLP fundamentals
- Experience shipping ML systems in cloud environments (AWS / GCP / Azure)
- Strong communication skills; comfortable working cross-functionally with recruiters and PMs

Nice to have:
- Experience with BentoML or similar model-serving frameworks
- Background in HR-tech, talent marketplaces, or two-sided matching platforms
- Contributions to open-source ML / IR libraries

Location: Pune or Noida preferred; open to strong candidates elsewhere in India willing to relocate.
Work mode: Hybrid.
